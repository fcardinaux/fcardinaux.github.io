Open-weight AI models for the lucky owners of 128 GB of unified memory: what fits, what runs, and what the people who actually use them say.

Every model is judged against the same 128 GB, so "fits" and "too large" mean something. Language, vision, OCR, image, video, speech, music, translation, time-series and more.

For machines whose CPU and GPU share 128 GB: Apple silicon Macs first (MLX, llama.cpp, Ollama), then AMD Ryzen AI Max+ (Strix Halo) and NVIDIA DGX Spark, where the same size limits apply.
