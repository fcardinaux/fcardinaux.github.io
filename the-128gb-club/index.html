Open-weight AI models for the lucky owners of 128 GB of unified memory: what fits, what runs, and what the people who actually use them say.

Every model is judged against the same 128 GB, so "fits" and "too large" mean something. Language, vision, OCR, image, video, speech, music, translation, time-series and more.

For machines whose CPU and GPU share 128 GB: Apple silicon Macs first (MLX, llama.cpp, Ollama), then AMD Ryzen AI Max+ (Strix Halo) and NVIDIA DGX Spark, where the same size limits apply.

Model comparison

One record per model: licence, size, formats, publisher, release date, runtimes, and short summaries of user feedback, always with the link and the date.

Taxonomies

Image, music, speech, documents, vision and 3D, retrieval, code, video and time series: what each kind of model does, and the main open candidates.

Glossary

Terms, software and organisations, with French, German and Italian translations.

Research method

Where facts and feedback come from, how recent they must be, and how third-party conversions are checked.

Principles: facts from the official source, dated; feedback from people who ran the model, summarised in our own words with the link; open licences and safe formats (safetensors, GGUF) shown first; no model weights in the repository.
